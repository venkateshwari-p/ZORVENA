## Caution
A coir-using grower may not currently be buying new cocopeat from overseas; some farms reuse or recycle substrates. Treat these as relevant prospect candidates, not guaranteed purchasing leads. Verify legal entity, current location, duplicate/brand relationships, and permitted use of sources before import. Do not add personal contact details from this research batch.
